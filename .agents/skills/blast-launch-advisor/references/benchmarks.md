# Industry precedents

Read when the team asks for industry standards, comparable allocations or vesting guidance. **There is no universal standard percentage or vesting term.** Use these as dated examples, then find closer peers for the team's sector, stage, utility, funding history and distribution method. A mature protocol's airdrop is not a comparable presale just because both issued tokens.

Sources below were checked **27 October 2026**. Reopen relevant primary sources before using them; these notes describe published designs, not current balances, returns or a representative market sample.

| Precedent | Observed design | What it helps discuss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Uniswap's September 2020 UNI announcement](https://blog.uniswap.org/uni) | Initial allocation: community 60%, team/future employees 21.266%, investors 18.044%, advisors 0.69%; team, investors and advisors had four-year vesting. 15% of supply was immediately claimable by historical users/LPs. | Community includes immediately claimable and reserved programs; distinguish allocation from float. A four-year commitment is a precedent, not a required Blast setting. UNI's later inflation also differs from Blast's sealed supply. |
| [Arbitrum distribution](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/airdrop-eligibility-distribution) and [unlock explanation](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/token-supply) | Team/contributors/advisors 26.94%, investors 17.53% in the published post-AIP distribution. Team/contributor/investor unlocks span four years from March 2023; first unlock March 2024, then monthly. | A real example for insider concentration and cliff/unlock analysis. Its token had no public presale; this is a vesting/allocation comparison, not evidence for choosing a fundraising mechanism. |
| [Sui tokenomics documentation](https://docs.sui.io/develop/sui-architecture/tokenomics-overview) | SUI has distinct gas, staking and governance roles; the initial investor cliff was one year, ending May 2024. | Tie token demand to a specific system role. A base-chain design does not establish demand or appropriate allocations for a game or community token. |
| [Raydium LaunchLab vesting](https://docs.raydium.io/products/launchlab/vesting) | Optional reserved supply with a cliff after fundraising and a subsequent linear unlock period. Its team-allocation percentages are examples, not market statistics. | A nearby launch-product precedent for enforceable contributor reserves. Its post-cliff accrual differs from Blast presale's catch-up cliff; its native feature does not establish Blast curve support. |

When making a recommendation:

1. Select relevant precedents and say why they fit or fail to fit. Verify specific allocation and schedule claims from the project's own docs, announcement, governance or deployed contract. If peers are missing, say the proposal is goal-derived rather than industry-validated.
2. Label each statement as an observed precedent, an inference from comparisons, or the proposed design. Explain any recommended range through runway, incentive duration, float and concentration; do not turn one peer into a market median.
3. Translate the intended economic promise into Blast's actual primitives. A “12-month cliff plus three-year vest” from another system may have a different first unlock, anchor, cancelability or total duration.
4. Cite sources next to material claims, record the date checked, and retain both supporting and disconfirming examples. Use authoritative jurisdiction-specific sources if the team raises legal eligibility or fundraising requirements; a token allocation convention establishes no legal status.
